# Creative direction

**THE MEGA MYSTERIOUS MURDER OF R-3MI** · *A Completely Non-Canon Team_Aperture Mystery*

> Flight 303, night. Everyone is asleep, and so is R-3MI. A detective who has never solved a case is about
> to solve one that never happened.

## Pillars

1. **Character first.** Every scene exists so that R-3MI, V-TGM and ICH can act. Puzzles and rooms serve the acting.
2. **The face that isn't.** ICH has no face, the robots have one eye each. All three are forced to act with
   bodies, hands and light. That restriction is the house style, not a limitation to hide.
3. **Timing over volume.** Fewer gags, timed properly: anticipation, the hold, the payoff, the reaction to the payoff.
4. **A loving spin-off.** Canon characters stay themselves (see [CHARACTER_BIBLE.md](CHARACTER_BIBLE.md));
   only the situation is absurd.
5. **Fair play.** The mystery cheats nobody. Every twist is planted on screen and in the sound mix,
   and nothing contradicts it.

## Look

- **2D cartoon, not pixel art.** Clean shapes, coloured ink outlines of varying weight, two-tone cel shading
  plus a soft rim light. Readable silhouettes first, detail second.
- **Shape language.** V-TGM = circle, R-3MI = vertical line/egg, ICH = rectangle on a triangle,
  the cabin = repeated soft rectangles (seats, windows, bins) so the characters always contrast with it.
- **Colour.** The cabin at night is deep blue and violet. Warm amber pools from reading lights. Characters
  carry their own light: green, red, amber. Nothing else in the cabin is as saturated as those three.
- **Lighting.** Characters are tinted by the cabin light at their position (darker between rows, warm under
  a reading light), while eyes, letters and LEDs stay at full brightness and cast glow onto nearby surfaces.
- **Staging.** A side cut-away of the cabin. The aisle is the stage floor, seats face left (towards the
  cockpit), windows and bins form the back wall, dark seat-back silhouettes slide past in the foreground.
  All characters are drawn in a cheated three-quarter view, the classic stage convention of cut-out animation.
- **Camera.** A real camera with weight: it eases, leads the walking character, frames groups in dialogue,
  pushes in for punchlines and holds still for deadpan. No shake to fake impact; one small, honest jolt is
  allowed when something heavy actually hits the floor.

## Sound

- A soft, constant cabin hum, a chime that means "announcement", rain on the fuselage.
- Each character has a synthesised "voice" (no recorded speech): R-3MI bright and quick, V-TGM low and clipped,
  ICH pompous and sliding. The voice follows the text as it appears.
- A small noir-jazz motif for ICH (walking bass, brushes, vibraphone), and a three-note sting for his
  "dramatic" moments, which undercuts itself.

## Language

- All UI and narration in German. The main title stays English.
- R-3MI and ICH speak German. V-TGM speaks English with a German subtitle (canon rule, see the reference guide).
- ICH speaks in confident, slightly formal German and calls himself ICH, which produces sentences like
  „ICH bin ICH." He considers this a complete introduction.

## Chapter 0: „Boarding abgeschlossen"

| # | Beat | What it proves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Title sequence.** A split-flap board flips to FLUG 303. Title letters drop in with squash and stretch. ICH struts in, poses, and gets flattened by the word MURDER. | rig, walk cycle, timing, typography |
| 2 | **Cabin opening.** Slow pan through the sleeping cabin, rain on the windows, the captain's announcement. | atmosphere, camera, background life |
| 3 | **ICH's entrance.** The lavatory sign flips to FREI, the door bursts open, backlit hero pose, the door swings back and hits him. Player control. | playable ICH, strut walk, controls |
| 4 | **Meet R-3MI and V-TGM.** Introductions („ICH." „Ja, aber wer?"), a dialogue choice with three different acted responses. | dialogue presentation, acting, choices |
| 5 | **The seat.** R-3MI tries three ways to sit, loses to physics and to V-TGM's recline, complains, enters deep-sleep mode. His eye goes dark. | articulated acting, comic escalation |
| 6 | **Investigation.** ICH "investigates" although there is no crime yet („NOCH nicht."). Five objects, at least three needed. Each gets an absurd deduction. One is accidentally right. | investigation mechanic |
| 7 | **Physical comedy.** The overhead-bin avalanche. Tripping over R-3MI's legs in the aisle. | slapstick timing |
| 8 | **Teaser.** Lights dim. ICH sleeps with his hat on his plate. A shadow, a click, darkness. A scream: R-3MI has been murdered. Lights on. To be continued. | cinematic ending |

The player sees R-3MI fall asleep. The teaser deliberately adds doubt: something happens while
everybody sleeps, and in the morning his deep-sleep lights are out too. Was he only sleeping? The game
will not say until the end.
