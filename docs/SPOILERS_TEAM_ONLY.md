## Twist 1: R-3MI is not dead, he is asleep

**What Chapter 0 plants**
- R-3MI announces that he is going into *Tiefschlafmodus*, and that in deep sleep he notices „absolut gar nichts".
- The power-down is shown in full: lids close, the eye dims to black, the tank bubbles slow down, the chest
  slot light starts a very slow standby blink (≈ one pulse every 4 s).
- In the teaser the standby blink is still there for a moment, then stops (deeper sleep stage), so the
  later night scene shows a fully dark R-3MI. A later chapter can reveal that the blink stops in deep sleep and
  restarts on its own.

**What Chapter 0 adds as doubt** (so the player does not simply conclude "he is asleep")
- A shadow passes along the aisle during the night. A metallic click. Nobody in the prototype explains it.
- The scream comes from an unseen voice. Who declares the murder is left open on purpose.

altitude announcement. The only "takeoff" in the game will be the imagined one in the normal ending.
